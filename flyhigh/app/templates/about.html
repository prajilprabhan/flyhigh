Established in 2024, Fly High Study Abroad Pvt. Ltd. has emerged as a leading name in overseas education consultancy. Headquartered in Kerala, India, we are guided by our visionary founder and Managing Director, Mr. Rajesh Kapoor, an esteemed educationist, author, motivational speaker, and philanthropist. Our mission is to offer comprehensive study abroad facilitation services that empower students to achieve their international academic goals. br We proudly represent over 500 prestigious Universities and Colleges across 25+ countries, providing our clients with access to world-class educational opportunities. With branches and associate offices spread across major Indian cities and several international locations, Fly High Study Abroad ensures that our services are within reach for aspiring students from all corners.
At Fly High Study Abroad, we have built our reputation on the pillars of quality and reliability. Our team of highly skilled professionals, with extensive international exposure, is dedicated to guiding students through every step of their study abroad journey. From counseling and admissions to visa assistance and pre-departure orientations, we offer end-to-end support to ensure a smooth transition for our students.
Our success is driven by our commitment to excellence, continuous staff development, and the integration of cutting-edge technology. Above all, we adhere to a “student-first” approach, ensuring that the needs and aspirations of our clients are always our top priority.
